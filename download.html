❓ Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you download.

What is NMC revalidation?

Revalidation is the process UK nurses go through every three years to confirm they remain fit to practise. It involves meeting requirements around CPD hours, practice hours, reflective accounts, and feedback from colleagues.

Does Revalidation Copilot store my data?

Your data stays on your device and in your own cloud account. Revalidation Copilot does not submit anything to the NMC on your behalf. You export and share your portfolio with your confirmer when you are ready.

How much does it cost?

The app is free to download and use. Get full dashboard access, unlimited AI-generated reflective accounts, practice hour tracking, and NMC-ready portfolio exports. No monthly fees — ever.

What are the NMC revalidation requirements?

The NMC requires 35 hours of CPD (including 20 participatory hours), 450 practice hours, five reflective accounts, and five pieces of practice-related feedback every three years. Revalidation Copilot helps you track all of these.

Which platforms are supported?

Revalidation Copilot is available on iOS and Android. Download from the Apple App Store or Google Play.
