Feedback request

Someone has asked you for feedback

Every nurse, midwife and nursing associate on the NMC register needs five pieces of feedback about their practice every three years. It can come from anyone who has seen them work — colleagues, managers, other professionals, students, or the people they have cared for and their families.

A few honest sentences is all that's needed, and it takes about two minutes. There's no account to create, no password, and nothing to pay.

Please don't include patient names, dates of birth, NHS numbers, or anything else that could identify a patient.
